# Workbox

- Serve apps as production builds: `host-check pnpm build`, then restart the
  mapped `*-prod` service.
- Previews (any browser or visual check of dcaid, receipt-hub, or
  nj-homes-choice[-next]): read `~/.config/workbox/previews.md` first.
- NJHOMES feature flags: read `~/.config/workbox/njhomes-flags.md` first.
- To merge, ask Pedro to reply `merge <N>` naming each PR; the merge guard
  holds every merge until he does.
